The program

DraftEasy is free for private and commercial use. You may pass on the unchanged installer free of charge, but not sell it, change it or hand it out under another name. The full licence is shown when you install the program. DraftEasy comes without warranty: keep backups of your drawings.

Support payments

  • Paying is voluntary. The program is the same whether you pay or not, and a payment buys no product, licence, service or support.
  • You choose the amount. Payments are made in Norwegian kroner (NOK); your bank may convert from your own currency.
  • Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments are processed by Stripe; other payments by Revolut. I never see your card details.
  • Card statements show the payment as DRAFTEASY.

Refunds

If you paid by mistake or the wrong amount, tell me within 14 days through the feedback form, with the date and amount, and I refund the full amount the same way it was paid. Otherwise payments are not refunded, as they are voluntary gifts.

Privacy

  • The program collects no data and sends nothing anywhere, except when you install ODA File Converter or open a link.
  • This site uses no cookies and no analytics. Your language choice is saved in your own browser.
  • The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as with any web request.
  • When you pay, the payment provider (Stripe or Revolut) handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. I receive your name, the amount and the date, and keep them only as long as the accounting rules require.
  • The feedback form is run by Tally (Belgium, EU). It does not ask for your e-mail address.
